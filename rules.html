Season I · The rulebook

The Rules

Two things govern a Hidden Cup: the Rule of the Mask, which keeps every lord nameless until the crown is settled, and the Terms of the Siege, the ruleset the host has set for the field itself. Both are below in full.

How it is fought

The Rule of the Mask

A Hidden Cup is decided by play alone. Reputation, ladder rank and rivalry are left at the gate.

  1. I
    Concealed identities The qualifiers are fought openly, under real gamertags. From the main event onward every lord is assigned an alias — it is not chosen — and must set their Stronghold 2 in-game name to it. Which alias belongs to whom is withheld from casters, chat and audience until the reveal.
  2. II
    Blind draw Seeding is drawn by lot before the aliases are assigned, so no lord can be inferred from their position in the bracket.
  3. III
    Neutral broadcast No name is ever spoken or written — not in the lobby, not in chat, not on the recording. Voices are never heard.
  4. IV
    Never ask, never tell No lord may ask another which alias is theirs, and none may give their own away. Keep the mystery alive — it is the only thing this tournament is built on.
  5. V
    Silence until the end Record your own point of view all you like, for your own channel. Nothing may be published or streamed — not a clip, not a VOD, not a live broadcast — until the whole tournament is over.
  6. VI
    The unmasking When the crown is settled, every mask comes off at once — press the Reveal identities button on the bracket to see who was behind each shield.

Terms of the Siege

Sixteen lords, single elimination, best of five. Everything below is the ruleset as the host has set it — the shape of a series, the terms each lord may call, the laws of the field, and what is owed to the victor.

Laws of the field

Break one and the host may take the game, or the series.

  1. No movement glitches Water-line and wood-line troop movement exploits are forbidden.
  2. No disbanding peasants for population Disbanding to push your population past the standard limit is strictly prohibited.
  3. Peace time is a border No crossing enemy or neutral land before peace time has expired.
  4. Fair play No hacks, no cheats, no exploits beyond what is agreed as standard in pro play.
  5. Enforcement A violation may cost the match or the whole series. The host reserves the right to add or adjust rules as the tournament requires.

What you must bring

Turn up without these and the siege cannot be fought.

  • Stronghold 2
    On the latest unofficial patch — both sides run the same version, checked at muster.
  • Discord
    Every draw, lobby and score report goes through it.
  • The ability to stream in Discord
    Your games are watched and recorded from your own screen share.
  • A strong, steady connection
    A dropped host mid-siege is a lost siege.

Player checklist

The mask is only as good as the routine around it. Both lists, every series.

Before a series

  1. Log in to the Discord tournament account you were given.
  2. Set your Steam friends list to private.
  3. Change your in-game name to your assigned alias — before you join the host, not after.
  4. In the lobby, discuss map, start gold and peace time only.
  5. In game, no chat beyond “gg!”.
  6. All lords must die at the end, so the end-game statistics come out right.

After a series

  1. Log out of the Discord tournament account.
  2. Change your in-game name back to your usual gamertag.
  3. Set your Steam friends list back to public.

And never

  1. Ask another player which alias is theirs, or tell anyone which is yours.
  2. Publish or stream a single frame of your own footage before the tournament is over.
