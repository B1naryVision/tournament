Single elimination · eleven lords

The Road to the Keep

Eleven banners rode in, and a bracket wants sixteen. Five lords are drawn a bye and pass the outer ward without a siege; the remaining six fight three opening sieges for the last three places in the quarter-finals. From there it is the same road for everyone — one defeat and out, winners marching right toward the keep, best of three all the way, best of five for the final and for the third-place siege. A short field is paid for here: reach the crown from an opening siege and you will have fought one series more than the lord who was handed a bye.

Any match marked Watch opens its full recording here on the page. Results go up as they are reported; the recordings follow once the whole tournament is over, and every lord stays masked until you choose otherwise.

Season I · The reserve list

Pledge Your Banner

Season I rides with eleven lords, and that field is closed. Eleven is short of a full bracket, so there are no qualifiers — every banner that was pledged goes straight into the draw, and the five byes make up the difference. Enlistment stays open all the same: pledge now and you stand on the reserve list, first called if a lord withdraws before the opening sieges, and first in the muster when Season II is drawn. Your alias is not yours to pick — it is assigned once the field is settled, and from that moment it is the only name the audience sees until the crown is decided.

Required

Install the Stronghold 2 Unofficial Patch before you play. Every lord must be on the latest release — it fixes a long list of the crash bugs that end multiplayer games early, and an unpatched client is grounds to forfeit.

Download the latest release from GitHub — B1naryVision/sh2-unofficial-patch. Both sides run the same version, checked at muster.
